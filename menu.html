A Taste of Freedom

Our Independence Day Signature Dish

Haiti's cherished pumpkin soup, traditionally enjoyed on January 1 to celebrate the nation's independence in 1804. More than a meal, Soup Joumou represents freedom, resilience, and Haitian pride.
