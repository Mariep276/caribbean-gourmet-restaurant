Our Story

Every remarkable dining experience begins with a story. Ours is rooted in the warmth of Caribbean hospitality, the richness of Haitian traditions, and a passion for bringing people together through unforgettable cuisine.

Where It All Began

Established in 2018

Caribbean Gourmet Restaurant opened its doors in 2018 with a simple dream: to create a destination where authentic Caribbean flavors could be enjoyed in an atmosphere of elegance, comfort, and genuine hospitality.

Inspired by the vibrant culture of Haiti and the beautiful traditions of the Caribbean, our restaurant was imagined as more than a place to dine. It was created as a welcoming retreat where every visit feels like a special occasion.

The Heart of Our Restaurant

Tradition Meets Elegance

From the inviting beauty of our dining room to the carefully prepared dishes served at every table, Caribbean Gourmet celebrates the connection between culinary heritage and refined dining.

Our menu honors beloved Haitian favorites while embracing the diverse flavors of the islands. Every meal reflects our commitment to quality, creativity, and the joy of sharing good food with family and friends.

Our Invitation to You

More Than a Restaurant

Caribbean Gourmet Restaurant is a celebration of culture, connection, and timeless island hospitality. Whether you join us for an intimate dinner or a memorable gathering, we invite you to become part of our continuing story.
